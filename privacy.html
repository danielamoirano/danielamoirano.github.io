Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit this website. Personal data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable person, such as your IP address. This website is a personal academic website. It does not use cookies, tracking, analytics or contact forms, and it does not load any content from third-party servers.

I. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:
Daniela Moirano
Email: daniela.moirano@gmail.com

II. Hosting by GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit this website, your browser automatically transmits technical information to GitHub's servers, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub.

This processing is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing a functioning and secure website). Data may be processed in the USA. GitHub states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Further information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement and in the GitHub Pages documentation.

III. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics or tracking tools. Fonts and icons are stored on this website itself, so no connection is made to external font services such as Google Fonts.

IV. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process the information you provide (such as your name, email address and the content of your message) in order to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries) or, where your enquiry relates to a potential employment or collaboration, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Your data will be deleted once it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

V. Links to other websites

This website contains links to external websites, such as LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ORCID and the Technical University of Munich. No data is transmitted to these providers when you visit this website. Only when you click a link does your browser connect to the respective provider, whose own privacy policy then applies.

VI. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data concerning you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise these rights, please contact me at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany).
